Our classes are designed to help students develop strong foundations in Kuchipudi while building confidence, discipline, musicality, and artistic expression.

Class Levels

Beginner

Perfect for students who are new to Kuchipudi or are beginning their classical dance journey.

Focus: Basic steps, posture, rhythm, hand gestures, and introductory choreography.

Recommended Ages: 5–9

Enroll →

Intermediate

Designed for students who have experience with Kuchipudi and are ready to develop their technique.

Focus: Advanced steps, expressions, choreography, rhythm, and performance skills.

Recommended Ages: 9–13

Enroll →

Advanced

For experienced students looking to strengthen their performance and artistic abilities.

Focus: Complex choreography, storytelling, abhinaya, stage presence, and performance.

Recommended Ages: 13+

What Will You Learn?

Technique

Develop strong foundations in posture, footwork, movement, and coordination.

Expression

Learn how facial expressions and gestures help bring stories and characters to life.

Rhythm

Build an understanding of tala, musicality, timing, and rhythmic patterns.

Performance

Develop confidence and stage presence through recitals, cultural events, and performances.

Performance Opportunities

Students have opportunities to showcase what they have learned through annual recitals, cultural celebrations, community events, and other performances throughout the year.
